## About these guides

These guides describe **Vectory 0.1.0-dev** with **Vector 0.58.0**. They are bundled with your installation, including search. For native component semantics beyond the platform workflows, see the [Vector configuration reference](https://vector.dev/docs/reference/configuration/). That external reference may describe a newer Vector release.
