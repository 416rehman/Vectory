# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users respectfully. Critique work with evidence. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, disclose private information, or use project channels for unwanted personal contact. Maintainers may remove harmful content and restrict participation to protect the community, explaining decisions when safe. Report conduct incidents privately to [416rehman@ahmadz.ai](mailto:416rehman@ahmadz.ai). Security vulnerabilities belong in the [private vulnerability reporting channel](SECURITY.md#report-a-vulnerability).
