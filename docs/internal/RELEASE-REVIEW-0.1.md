Fresh qualification and publication evidence belongs in [the release record](RELEASE-0.1.md). This review does not substitute an earlier workflow run for the final branch, main or tag checks.

The candidate's generated third-party license inventory records declared license expressions; it is not a review of each dependency's notice, attribution, source-offer or redistribution obligations. The source lockfile SPDX document also records `NOASSERTION` for packages whose lockfile does not declare a license. Before broad commercial promotion or downstream redistribution, review the actual shipped dependencies and bundled Vector-derived catalog and artwork against their license terms, including whether build-only packages enter the final images. CI verifies the presence and shape of the inventory, not legal clearance.
